# ANIL X — Store Release Gate

## Google Play
- Package: ai.anilx.app
- New submissions from 2026-08-31 must target Android 16 / API 36 or higher.
- New personal Play developer accounts created after 2023-11-13 need 12 continuously opted-in closed testers for 14 days before production access.
- Release signing/upload key and Play Console account are owner-controlled secrets.

## Apple App Store
- Bundle ID: ai.anilx.app
- Privacy Policy: /privacy.html
- Support: /support.html
- Marketing: /
- Screenshots, age rating, privacy answers and App Review information must be completed in App Store Connect.
- Apple requires the app to provide utility beyond a repackaged website; ANIL X therefore uses a native Capacitor shell plus bundled web assets and mobile integration.

## What cannot be automated without owner account access
- Creating/verifying the Apple Developer and Google Play developer accounts.
- Accepting store legal agreements.
- Registering the final bundle/package if not already registered.
- Upload signing keys/certificates and Apple provisioning.
- Final store submission and answering identity/tax/banking questions.

## Revenue settlement
Crypto receipts can be verified on-chain, then the owner can send supported assets to a compliant exchange, sell to Toman, and withdraw to a same-name card/IBAN when the exchange and jurisdiction permit it. ANIL X never asks for or stores seed phrases/private keys and does not automatically execute irreversible transfers.
