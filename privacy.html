ANIL X Privacy Policy

ANIL X is a global AI workspace for business, research, creation and execution.

Data

Depending on features used, ANIL X may process account information, requests, files or images, technical diagnostics, payment/order status and optional profile preferences. Payment credentials, seed phrases and private wallet keys are not collected by the public chat.

Third parties

Requests may be routed to configured AI or service providers when enabled. Payment processing is handled by configured providers.

Security

ANIL X does not request or store seed phrases or private keys. Irreversible financial actions require explicit owner approval.

Your choices

Use the support page for correction or deletion requests. Do not send passwords, private keys, seed phrases or full banking credentials through chat.
