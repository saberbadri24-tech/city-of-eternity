Settlement Hub

Owner-controlled preparation for converting received crypto or fiat into a bank settlement. ANIL X does not automatically sell, transfer or withdraw funds.

Crypto → Toman

  1. Verify the received transaction on-chain.
  2. Choose an exchange legally available to you and supporting the exact asset and network.
  3. Confirm the deposit address and network before sending.
  4. Sell the asset for Toman.
  5. Withdraw to your same-name bank account using the exchange's supported card or IBAN method.

Never send TON/USDT until the exchange confirms the exact supported network and address.

USD/fiat → Toman

  1. Confirm the payment is settled.
  2. Use a compliant payment or FX provider available to your jurisdiction.
  3. Complete required verification.
  4. Withdraw to your verified bank account.

Bank details safety

This page does not send or store your card number or IBAN. Enter them only inside the regulated exchange or payment provider you choose.
